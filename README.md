# Possible Mission

A fan-made tribute to *Impossible Mission* (Epyx, 1984) for the browser, in the look of the Commodore 64 original, with a much bigger stronghold to explore.

**Play it here: https://deepspace000.github.io/Possible-mission/**

## A tribute, not a product

Possible Mission is a free, non-commercial fan prototype, made as a tribute to one of the great Commodore 64 games. It is not for sale, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Epyx or the owners of *Impossible Mission*. The original game's name, characters and graphics belong to their owners. If you hold the rights and would like anything changed or taken down, please open an issue.

## Play the original

This is only a tribute. If you enjoy it, go and play the real *Impossible Mission* on a Commodore 64, or on an official re-release.

## Playing

Press Space or tap the screen to start. On an iPad or iPhone, open the link in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**: the game gets its own icon and opens full screen, with on-screen controls.

| Keyboard | Joypad | iPad | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Left / Right (or A / D) | D-pad or stick | D-pad | Run |
| Up / Down (or W / S) | D-pad or stick | D-pad | Ride lifts and elevators, climb ladders |
| Hold Up | Hold up | Hold SEARCH | Search furniture, use a terminal or a train |
| Space | A | JUMP | Somersault, in rooms and in the elevator hallways |
| F or X | B | FIRE | Shoot, once you have found the gun |
| C or Enter | Y | PUZZLE | In an elevator, open or close the puzzle window |
| Tab | Select | MAP | Show the whole map, in an elevator or a room |
| P | Start | PAUSE | Pause |
| M | | SOUND | Sound on or off |
| Esc | | BACK | Back to the game from the map, the puzzle or any console |
